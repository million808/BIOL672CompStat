Biological Significance of ANCOVA:
ANCOVA allows us to separate treatment effects from natural variation due to size or surface area.
This is important because larger fish or those with more surface area might naturally have different villi dimensions.
By controlling for these factors, we can see the "pure" treatment effects on morphology.
