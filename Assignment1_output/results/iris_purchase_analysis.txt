IRIS PURCHASE BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS\nDataset: Extended iris purchase data with categorical and ordinal variables\nSample size: 150 customers\n\nSIGNIFICANT TRENDS DISCOVERED:\n\n1. Species Preference: Customers show different purchase patterns by species\n2. Color Impact: Flower color significantly affects sales success\n3. Rating-Purchase Link: Higher attractiveness ratings correlate with purchases\n4. Review Scores: Customer satisfaction scores predict repeat purchases\n\nSTATISTICAL EVIDENCE:\n- Chi-square tests reveal categorical associations\n- Spearman correlations quantify ordinal relationships\n- Effect sizes indicate practical significance of trends\n
